The Reports page is the archive. The Site Audit and AI Visibility overviews preview only their three most recent runs — this is where every run lives.

{/* IMAGE: Reports page */}

## Site picker

One picker at the top scopes all three tabs to a site. Workspaces with a single site default to it.

## Monthly Reports

A generated summary of the month's performance, designed to be shared with people who don't log in. It covers:

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Site Health** | Score and movement over the month |
| **AI Visibility** | GEO score and per-platform visibility |
| **AI Prompts You Win** | Prompts where an AI recommends you, and the platforms doing it |
| **Prompt Movement** | Prompts gained, held and lost |
| **Prompt Mention Trend** | Mention rate across the month's runs |
| **Google Search Console** | Clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, top queries, pages, countries and devices |
| **Domain Authority Growth** | Authority score over time |
| **Backlink Profile** | Backlinks and referring domains, with gained and lost |
| **Google Ranking Improvements** | Keywords tracked and position changes |

Reports can be downloaded as PDF. Your first monthly report appears once there's a full month of data to summarise.

<Note>
  Search Console sections need [Google Search Console connected](/workspace/integrations). Without it, those panels are omitted.
</Note>

## AI Visibility (GEO) history

Every GEO run for the selected site, newest first — score, date and status. Open any run to read the report exactly as it was, or compare it against another run.

## Site Audit history

Every Site Audit run for the selected site — overall score, issue count, date and status, with the same open-and-compare behaviour.

<Tip>
  Run history is how you prove impact. Publish a fix, re-run, and compare the two reports side by side.
</Tip>
