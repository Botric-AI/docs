Both feed the [Analytics](/workspace/analytics) page, and Search Console also enriches your [monthly reports](/workspace/reports).

## Connecting Google Search Console

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Connect">
    Select **Google Search Console** and authorise Botric with the Google account that owns the property.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the property">
    Botric matches your workspace domain to a verified Search Console property. If nothing matches, you're told which domain it looked for — verify that property in Search Console and retry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Once connected, the GSC tab in Analytics fills in. Use **Sync now** to pull fresh data on demand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connecting Google Analytics

Authorise Botric with the Google account that has access to the GA4 property, then select the property to link. A connection can sit in a pending state until a property is chosen — the page tells you when that's the case.

<Tip>
  The **See Agents Traffic** task on your Get Started checklist links straight here with GA4 pre-selected.
</Tip>

## Managing connections

Each connected integration shows its status and offers **Sync now** and **Disconnect**. Disconnecting stops data collection immediately; historical data already pulled stays in your reports.

## Plan requirements

Integrations are gated by plan. Google Analytics shares the Search Console entitlement. When your plan doesn't include an integration, its tile shows an **Upgrade** badge linking to [Plans](/account/plans).
