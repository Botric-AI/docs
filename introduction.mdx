Search moved. People now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews instead of scanning ten blue links — and those engines answer from a handful of sources they trust. Botric measures whether your brand is one of them, tells you exactly why it isn't, and does the work to change that.

## How the product is organised

Everything you do happens inside a **workspace** — one brand, one website. The sidebar groups a workspace's tools the same way this documentation does:

| Section | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | Dashboard, Brand Profile, Knowledge Source, Reports, Analytics, Integrations |
| **Search** | [Site Audit](/search/site-audit), [GEO Audit](/search/geo-audit) |
| **Discoverability** | [AI Visibility (GEO)](/discoverability/ai-visibility/overview), [Prompt Research](/discoverability/prompt-research), [Citations](/discoverability/citations), [Competitors](/discoverability/competitors) |
| **Content** | [Blog Agent](/content/blog-agent), [Page Agent](/content/page-agent) |
| **Customer Support** | [Chat Agent](/support/chat-agent/overview), [Conversations](/support/conversations) |

Account-level settings — profile, team, plans, credits and invoices — sit outside any workspace under **Settings**. See [Account & Billing](/account/profile).

## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.botric.ai](https://app.botric.ai) with a one-time email code or your Google account. No password to remember. See [Account setup](/getting-started/account-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run onboarding">
    A four-step wizard reads your website, drafts your brand profile, finds your competitors and picks the pages to audit. See [Onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work the Get Started checklist">
    Six tasks take you from a fresh workspace to a live chat agent and your first AI-optimized blog post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your first reports">
    Site Audit and AI Visibility both run automatically at the end of onboarding. Your scores are waiting on the workspace dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer a guided tour? Ved, our founder, runs 15-minute onboarding calls for new accounts — [book a slot](https://calendly.com/botric-ai/30min). Most customers get fully live faster with a quick call.
</Tip>
