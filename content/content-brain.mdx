Before the Blog Agent writes anything, it builds a **Content Brain**: a consolidated understanding of your business, your market and your current performance. Everything the agent produces is grounded in it.

{/* IMAGE: Content Brain build screen */}

## The four signals

The Brain is assembled from up to four inputs. The build screen shows which are present.

| Signal | Source | Contributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Site Audit** | [Site Audit](/search/site-audit) | Technical health, on-page issues and crawlability of your pages |
| **AI Visibility (GEO)** | [AI Visibility](/discoverability/ai-visibility/overview) | AI mentions, prompts, citations and content-coverage gaps |
| **SERP Intelligence** | Run on demand | Keyword worthiness and platform gaps that sharpen your content |
| **Search Console (GSC)** | [Integrations](/workspace/integrations) | Optional — real Google impressions and queries that further sharpen content |

<Note>
  A completed **Site Audit** is required. GEO and SERP make the output substantially better; Search Console is optional but is the only signal that reflects real Google demand rather than modelled demand.
</Note>

## What the Brain knows

It's organised into three views:

- **My Business** — who you are, what you sell, how you position it.
- **My Market** — who else competes, what your buyers ask, where the demand is.
- **My Progress** — what you've published, what's working, what's still open.

## Building it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your signals">
    The setup screen lists each signal and whether it's ready. Run anything missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your content preferences">
    Author name and content preferences shape tone and attribution. The author is pre-filled from your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build">
    The build runs through several stages with live progress. It takes a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discovery runs next">
    On a first build, topic discovery is chained automatically so you land on a set of ideas rather than an empty page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rebuilding

Rebuild after anything that changes your position: a fresh Site Audit, a new GEO run, a repositioning, or a batch of published content. The Brain reflects the signals as they were at build time.

**View context** shows exactly what the current Brain contains, so you can judge whether it's stale.

<Warning>
  A Brain built before your latest GEO run won't know about the gaps that run found. If your visibility data has moved meaningfully, rebuild before generating content.
</Warning>
