**Settings → Contact Support** opens a ticket directly with our team. It's also reachable from the **Support** link in the top bar.

{/* IMAGE: Contact support form */}

## Raising a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a problem type">
    **Technical**, **Billing**, **Account** or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a severity">
    **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Critical**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the workspace">
    Which workspace the issue relates to, if any.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    A brief **subject**, then a **description** with the detail. Add a **phone number** if you'd like a call back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Slide to confirm and the ticket is created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Writing a good ticket

The page's own quick tips, and they're the right ones:

- Be specific about your issue.
- Include relevant screenshots.
- Mention error messages if any.
- Provide steps to reproduce the problem.

<Tip>
  For a failed run, include the workspace name, the site, and roughly when it happened. That's usually enough for us to find the run without going back and forth.
</Tip>

## Tracking your tickets

**Previous tickets** lists everything you've raised with its current status — **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed** — and any replies from our team.
