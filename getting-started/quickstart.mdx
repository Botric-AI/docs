This is the fastest path from a new account to real data. Every step links to a fuller guide if you want the detail.

## 1. Create your account

Go to [app.botric.ai](https://app.botric.ai) and sign up with **Google** or with your **email address**. Email sign-up sends a six-digit code — there is no password.

<Card title="Account setup" icon="user-plus" href="/getting-started/account-setup">
  Sign-in methods, verification codes, and switching accounts.
</Card>

## 2. Complete onboarding

Onboarding is a four-step wizard. Give it your website URL and it does most of the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Website">
    Name the workspace, paste your website URL, and pick your target market (one country).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand">
    Botric reads your site and drafts your brand name, summary, up to five core topics and your brand colours. Correct anything that's wrong — every later agent reads this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Competitors">
    Botric finds competitors for each of your topics. Keep up to five, add your own, drop the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pages">
    Choose which of your site's pages the first audit should crawl.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you're a brand-new account you'll pick a plan straight after — free, or a paid plan with a 7-day trial. Your workspace and first runs are created once the plan is active.

<Card title="Onboarding, step by step" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/getting-started/onboarding">
  What each step collects and how to change it later.
</Card>

## 3. Let the first runs finish

The moment your workspace exists, Botric fires two jobs in parallel:

- **Site Audit** — crawls the pages you selected and scores technical health.
- **AI Visibility (GEO)** — asks your tracked prompts across the AI platforms on your plan and scores how often you're named and cited.

Both take a few minutes. You can leave the page; progress banners and the sidebar badges pick up where you left off.

## 4. Read your scores

Open **Dashboard** in the workspace sidebar. You get two headline numbers:

- **Site Health** — 0–100, from the latest Site Audit.
- **AI Visibility (GEO)** — 0–100, from the latest GEO run.

## 5. Work the Get Started checklist

**Get Started** in the sidebar tracks six tasks and shows your progress as a ring:

| Task | Where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| Run Site Audit | [Site Audit](/search/site-audit) |
| Review AI Visibility (GEO) | [AI Visibility](/discoverability/ai-visibility/overview) |
| Add Knowledge Sources | [Knowledge Source](/workspace/knowledge-source) |
| See Agents Traffic | [Integrations](/workspace/integrations) → connect GA4 |
| Launch Chat Agent | [Chat Agent](/support/chat-agent/overview) |
| Generate a Blog | [Blog Agent](/content/blog-agent) |

Steps that your plan doesn't include show an **Upgrade** button instead and link to [Plans](/account/plans).
