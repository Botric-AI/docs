The **Prompts** tab is the raw evidence behind your GEO score. For each prompt you see what every AI platform actually said, and where you landed.

## The four outcomes

Each prompt/platform pair resolves to one of four states. This is the vocabulary the whole report uses.

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Named and cited** | The brand appears in the answer and a brand URL is a source. No gap. |
| **Named, not cited** | The engine names the brand but sourced the answer elsewhere. **Content gap.** |
| **Cited, not named** | A brand page is a source but the brand goes unnamed. **Positioning gap.** |
| **Not mentioned** | Invisible for this prompt — no name in the answer, no brand URL in the sources. |

<Tip>
  *Named, not cited* is the most fixable state. The model already knows who you are — it just didn't have a page of yours worth quoting. That's a content problem with a clear answer.
</Tip>

## Filtering

Narrow the list by:

- **Platform** — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI, Mistral, Poe, AI Overview.
- **Outcome** — mentioned, not mentioned, cites you, cites a competitor, cites both, neither.
- **Intent type** — Informational, Commercial, Comparison, Buyer intent, Branded, Locational, Adjacent, Direct, General.

## Intent types

Every prompt is classified by what the person asking actually wants.

| Intent | Example shape |
| --- | --- |
| **Informational** | "What is …" |
| **Commercial** | "Best tools for …" |
| **Comparison** | "X vs Y" |
| **Buyer intent** | "Pricing for …", "How much does … cost" |
| **Branded** | Prompts naming your brand directly |
| **Locational** | "… in `<city>`" |
| **Adjacent** | Neighbouring problems your buyers have |
| **Direct** | Straightforward product questions |

Coverage across intent types matters as much as raw volume: winning every informational prompt while losing every comparison prompt is a losing position.

## Reading an answer

Open any prompt to see the full response from each platform, the sources it cited, which competitors it named, and the sentiment of your mention. **Draft reply** turns a weak answer into a content brief.

## Managing tracked prompts

Prompt Explorer, reachable from the toolbar, holds the tracked prompt set:

- **Tracked prompts** are generated and refilled by Botric to your plan's cap on every run.
- **Add-on prompts** are yours — fixed slots per site, available on paid plans. Locked slots show as **Locked**.

<Note>
  A run bills the full tracked cap plus your add-on prompts, regardless of how many prompts are currently visible. See [How credits work](/reference/credits).
</Note>

Saved prompts don't appear in results until the next run. When that happens, the overview shows a **stale prompts** banner.
